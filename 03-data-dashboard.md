## Data & Dashboard

### Data & Dashboard Analysis

This section presents a data-driven overview of **ABB's publicly available general statistics and business information**.

The analysis focuses on company-level indicators available through the ABB project website, including customer base, legal entities, employees, service offices, ATMs, financial indicators, products, business segments, and digital banking services.

The purpose of the dashboard is to transform these figures into a clear and accessible visual format, allowing users to quickly understand ABB's scale, business structure, service infrastructure, and digital capabilities.

### What the Dashboard Covers

* **Customer & Business Scale** — overview of individual customers and legal entities.
* **Workforce & Infrastructure** — employees, service offices, and ATM network.
* **Financial Indicators** — selected financial performance metrics presented on the source website.
* **Business Segments** — overview of ABB's major customer and business segments.
* **Banking Products & Services** — visualization of the major products and services offered.
* **Digital Banking** — overview of ABB's digital banking capabilities and channels.
* **Key Performance Indicators** — selected metrics converted into dashboard-friendly KPIs for easier interpretation.

### Analytical Approach

The dashboard does **not** attempt to estimate ABB's confidential customer behavior, churn rate, customer migration, or internal operational performance.

All displayed company-level figures are based on the information available through the project source website. The dashboard is therefore intended as a **Business Analysis and Data Visualization exercise**, demonstrating how publicly available business information can be structured, analyzed, and communicated to stakeholders.

### Key Takeaways

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of ABB's:

* Large-scale customer and business ecosystem
* Extensive physical service infrastructure
* Broad banking product portfolio
* Multiple business and customer segments
* Combination of traditional and digital banking channels
* Overall organizational and operational scale

These indicators provide the **business context** for the wider Business Analysis project and help establish a factual baseline before moving into process analysis, requirements, solution design, and KPI evaluation.
